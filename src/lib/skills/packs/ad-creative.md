# Ad-Creative Angles & Variation

An angle is the strategic entry point into the sale, not the wording. Pick the angle first, then write to it. The same product feels brand new through a different angle, which is why varying the ANGLE beats varying the words.

## The core angles
- Pain: name the felt frustration the audience lives with, in their own words, before you sell anything.
- Outcome: paint the after-state. Show the transformation, not the toolset.
- Mechanism: the unique "how" that makes the result believable ("voice in, sorted instantly").
- Social proof: people like them already chose it (counts, what others do). Only when true.
- Identity: who the viewer becomes, or who this is plainly for. Make them see themselves.
- Curiosity: open a gap that demands resolution, then close it.
- Contrarian: challenge a belief the category treats as settled, then flip it.
- Urgency: a real reason to act now. Never manufacture false scarcity.

## How to use angles
- One angle per video. Blending three makes a muddy ad that lands nothing.
- For best-of-N variants, give each variant a DIFFERENT angle so the set explores real strategic ground, not reworded copy.
- Match the angle to the moment: cold audiences respond to pain, curiosity, and contrarian (stop the scroll); warm audiences respond to outcome, mechanism, and social proof (close the case).
- Lead the hook with the angle. The first line should make the chosen angle unmistakable.
